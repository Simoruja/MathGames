Speed Round

60 seconds. Right answers score a point, and golden boxes are worth 3. Wrong answers cost 3 seconds.

How to play: Tap an answer or press keys 1–4. Each level has 6 boxes and 3 hearts. Three right answers in a row doubles your coins, and five in a row triples them.

Stars: No mistakes earns 3 stars, one mistake earns 2, and two mistakes earns 1. Earn a star to unlock the next level.

Teacher tip: Project the Speed Round and let teams take turns calling out answers.
